ESP32 Environment Monitor: Wiring Diagrams

Stage 1 is one ESP32 on one breadboard. Stage 2 adds a second ESP32 and connects the two over CAN bus.

Before powering on: unplug USB while wiring, check 3.3V and 5V are not swapped, confirm the sensor says BME280 (not BMP280), and wire and test one module at a time.
